Wake up, Neo. Bring the iconic Matrix digital code rain to your Android device with zero battery compromise.

Matrix Rain is an ultra-optimized Live Wallpaper engineered specifically for AMOLED/OLED displays and high-refresh screens (60Hz, 90Hz, 120Hz).

KEY FEATURES:
• 0% Standby Battery Drain: Halts rendering completely when your screen is turned off or when an application is open in the foreground.
• True OLED Black: Pure #000000 background keeps individual pixels turned off, saving battery while delivering infinite contrast.
• Extreme Customization: Tweak rain speed, density, stream length, glitch frequency, and character sizes.
• Interactive Touch Ripples: Tap and drag across your screen to send digital shockwaves through the code waterfall.
• Special Drops: Rare Red Virus streams, Gold Neo drops, and falling custom messages.
• Privacy First: 100% offline, zero trackers, zero analytics, zero ads.

Free & Open Source:
100% free and open source with all features unlocked. Check out the source code at https://github.com/kush-chou/matrixrain
