Wake up, Neo. Bring the iconic Matrix digital code rain to your Android device with zero battery compromise.

Matrix Rain is an ultra-optimized Live Wallpaper engineered specifically for AMOLED and OLED displays, folding screens, and high-refresh panels (60Hz, 90Hz, 120Hz).

KEY FEATURES:
• 6 Cyberpunk Color Themes: Choose between Classic Terminal Green, Neon Cyan, Crimson Virus, Amber Gold, Cyber Violet, and Monochrome Silver.
• True OLED Black (#000000): Pure black background keeps individual pixels turned off on AMOLED/OLED screens, saving battery while delivering infinite contrast.
• 0% Standby Battery Drain: Halts rendering completely when your screen is turned off or when an application is open in the foreground. Zero background CPU wake locks.
• Intelligent Battery Saver Mode: Automatically throttles frame pacing when Android Power Saver is active to maximize battery life while maintaining fluid motion.
• Special Drops & Cryptic Messages: Experience rare glowing Red Virus drops, Gold Neo streams, background depth layers, and falling hidden messages ("MATRIX", "SYSTEM", "WAKE UP", "NEO").
• Interactive Touch Shockwaves: Tap and drag across your screen to deflect and scatter falling glyphs with real-time radial shockwaves.
• Live-Sync Customization: Fine-tune glyph font size, cascade speed (from calm hypnotic trickle to high-velocity stream), character glitch frequency, and drop toggles with live preview sync.
• Privacy First: 100% offline. Zero network permissions requested, zero trackers, zero analytics, and zero ads.
