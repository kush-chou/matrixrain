Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

1. Overview

The Matrix Rain application respects and protects your privacy. This policy outlines our complete lack of data collection and our privacy-first design principles.

2. Data Collection and Usage

Matrix Rain does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data or usage analytics.

  • No Analytics: We do not use Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, Sentry, Mixpanel, or any third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No Ads: We do not serve advertising or integrate any advertising networks.
  • No Internet Permission: The application does not request the android.permission.INTERNET permission.

3. Permissions

Matrix Rain operates as an Android Live Wallpaper service (android.service.wallpaper.WallpaperService). It requires only the standard permissions strictly necessary to display graphics on your home screen.

4. Open Source Transparency

The complete source code for Matrix Rain is available for public audit and verification at:
https://github.com/kush-chou/matrixrain

5. Contact

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, please open an issue on the GitHub repository or contact the project maintainer.
